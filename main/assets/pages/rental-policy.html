ESSENTIAL CLAUSES

Key Rental Guidelines at a Glance

Read through the 6 core pillars of our vehicle lease and onboarding agreement.

Deposit & Weekly Recharge

Security deposit of ₹1,011/- payable during rider onboarding with ₹1,869/- weekly recharge. A ₹369/- service fee is charged, leaving a stated refundable balance of ₹1,500/-.

Mandatory Documents

Compulsory documents required before vehicle handover: Original Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, and Two Bank Cheques for verified digital KYC and fleet security.

Security Deposit Refund

Stated refund based on rental duration: Within 7 days: No refund; 45 days: ₹1,500/-; More than 45 days: ₹1,000/-, subject to inspection & deboarding terms.

JP Nagar Godown Deboarding

Vehicle pickup, onboarding, and deboarding must be completed at the designated 7th Phase JP Nagar Godown. Returning at any other hub forfeits the security deposit.

Roadside Assistance (RSA)

Roadside Assistance provided within a 6 km radius. If unresolved, vehicle must be transported to 7th Phase JP Nagar Godown via Porter (charges paid by rider).

Inspection & 7-Day Return

Mandatory photo/video inspection at handover and servicing. Applicable refunds and submitted documents are returned within 7 days after deboarding.

REFUND MATRIX

Duration-Based Security Deposit Refund

100% Transparent Terms

At onboarding, a security deposit of ₹1,011/- is collected. A standard ₹369/- service charge is applied, leaving a stated refundable balance of ₹1,500/-. The final refund released upon deboarding is governed by duration and compliance:

Rental Duration Stated Refund Amount Status & Settlement
Within 7 days No Refund Early deboarding prior to minimum 7-day cycle. Security deposit non-refundable.
45 days ₹1,500/- Full stated refundable balance credited via instant UPI within 7 days.
More than 45 days ₹1,000/- Long-term wear & continuous operational maintenance adjustment applied.

Hub Deboarding Requirement: Deboarding must be completed exclusively at the designated 7th Phase JP Nagar Godown. Leaving or returning the vehicle at any other battery swapping hub or unauthorized location results in forfeiture of the entire security deposit.

Weekly Billing Cycle & Timelines

The billing cycle commences immediately on the calendar date the rider is onboarded. The billing start date remains the same whether vehicle handover occurs in the morning or evening hours. Weekly recharge payments of ₹1,869/- must be completed on time to maintain active vehicle access and unlock automated battery swaps across all hubs.

Vehicle Inspection & Handover Protocol

Riders must record clear 360-degree photographs and high-resolution videos of the vehicle at the exact time of handover. The physical and mechanical condition of the electric scooter must also be photographed and logged whenever it is admitted for scheduled servicing or battery station inspection. Any pre-existing damage, scratches, or operational discrepancies must be communicated to the EXTER operations team immediately.

Roadside Assistance & Porter Transport Policy

On-road mobile assistance is provided within a 6 km radius of the designated 7th Phase JP Nagar Godown or applicable operating hub. If an on-road vehicle breakdown, tire failure, or electronic malfunction cannot be remediated on-site through RSA, the vehicle must be transported to the designated 7th Phase JP Nagar Godown via Porter logistics. All Porter vehicle transportation charges must be borne directly by the rider.

Non-Payment & Vehicle Recovery

Riders are required to pay weekly renewal charges promptly on or before the due date. In the event of recharge default or persistent payment delay, EXTER reserves the right to remotely immobilize the vehicle via smart telemetry and initiate immediate physical recovery. The security deposit will be forfeited in full to recover accrued rental dues and transport recovery costs.

Refund & Document Return Timeline

All stated refund amounts and submitted physical documents (including the two bank cheques) will be returned to the rider within 7 days following the verified deboarding date. Settlement is subject to final vehicle inspection, zero outstanding traffic fines/challans, and successful completion of the godown deboarding checklist.
