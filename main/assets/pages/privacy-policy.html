SECTION 01

Introduction & Corporate Scope

EXTER Energy Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (“EXTER”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is registered under the Companies Act, 2013, headquartered at 100 Feet Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038, Karnataka, India. We operate an integrated electric two-wheeler mobility ecosystem comprising smart commercial electric scooters, automated battery swapping stations, and rider management applications.

This Privacy Policy applies to all riders, fleet partners, battery swapping station hosts, and visitors interacting with our mobile applications, digital platforms, and physical charging infrastructure. We are fully committed to complying with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and all applicable Indian data privacy regulations.

SECTION 02

Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide seamless vehicle leasing, reliable battery swaps, emergency roadside assistance, and billing administration. This data is categorized into four distinct groups:

Identity & KYC Verification

Full name, mobile phone number, email address, government photo ID (Aadhaar or Voter ID), and valid Indian two-wheeler driving license to verify rider eligibility.

Financial & Deposit Records

Bank account details, UPI ID, and transaction receipts necessary for security deposit escrow (₹1,011), weekly subscription recharges (₹1,869/wk), and prompt refund disbursement.

Vehicle Telemetry & GPS

Vehicle coordinate logs during active rentals, battery state-of-charge (SoC), hub swap transaction timestamps, odometer tracking, and speed telemetry for safety alerts.

Device & App Diagnostics

Operating system version, mobile device model, IP address, crash logs, and session analytics gathered when utilizing the EXTER Riders Companion mobile application.

SECTION 03

How We Use Your Data

Your information is utilized solely to deliver, safeguard, and optimize your EXTER riding experience:

  • Rider Onboarding & Verification: Authenticating legal driving credentials and activating vehicle allocation within 2 hours of KYC completion.
  • Automated Swapping Infrastructure: Validating battery compatibility and unlocking swap docks at our 24/7 JP Nagar and Bengaluru hub locations in under 90 seconds.
  • 24/7 Roadside Assistance & Maintenance: Directing mobile mechanics to your exact coordinates when you report a puncture, electrical issue, or towing request.
  • Safety & Asset Protection: Monitoring vehicle health alerts, detecting sudden collisions or thermal anomalies, and preventing vehicle theft.
  • Statutory Compliance: Complying with Indian motor vehicle regulations, taxation reporting, and authorized law enforcement requests when accompanied by court order.
SECTION 04

Vehicle Telemetry & GPS Transparency

Every EXTER commercial electric scooter is equipped with a certified automotive IoT telematics gateway. Because we value your privacy, we handle telemetry data with extreme precision and clear boundaries:

Active Rental Transparency Guarantee

Telemetry and GPS tracking operate exclusively during active subscription periods for operational safety, battery state management, and 6 km godown RSA dispatch. EXTER never uses telemetry for personal behavior profiling, off-duty surveillance, or commercial resale.

Telemetry streams are separated from personal rider identities in our internal data warehouse using pseudonymized UUID tokens. Access to real-time vehicle GPS is strictly restricted to our 24/7 Fleet Operations Dispatch center during active emergency alerts.

SECTION 05

Data Security, Encryption & Retention

EXTER applies rigorous physical, administrative, and technical safeguards engineered to Apple and ISO/IEC 27001 data security standards:

  • End-to-End Encryption: All data in transit between IoT gateways, mobile devices, and cloud servers is secured via TLS 1.3. Stored database records are encrypted with AES-256.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Employee access to rider identity and financial data is limited to verified personnel with multi-factor authentication (MFA) and immutable audit logging.
  • Strict Retention & Automatic Purging: Telemetry and GPS coordinates are stored for 90 days for operational safety before being aggregated into anonymous statistical metrics. KYC records are retained only as long as required under the Motor Vehicles Act and tax guidelines.
SECTION 06

Your Data Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023), you hold clear and enforceable rights regarding how your digital personal data is handled by EXTER:

Right to Information & Summary

You may request a clear summary of all personal data held by EXTER and the identities of third parties with whom it has been shared.

Right to Correction & Update

Promptly rectify inaccurate, misleading, or outdated personal information, KYC documents, or bank details directly in the app.

Right to Erasure & Deletion

Request permanent erasure of your personal data when your subscription has concluded and statutory deposit hold periods have elapsed.

Right to Nominate

Nominate any individual to exercise your privacy and data rights in the unfortunate event of death or incapacity.
