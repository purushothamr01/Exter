2. Information We Collect

We may collect information in the following categories:

  • Identity & KYC Data: Name, phone number, email address, government identification (Aadhaar, Voter ID), and driving license details for rider authentication.
  • Financial Data: Bank account details and UPI addresses required for security deposit escrow and rental billing.
  • Telemetry & Geolocation: Vehicle GPS location during active rental periods, battery state-of-charge, swapping hub access logs, speed, and diagnostics necessary for rider safety and asset protection.
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and analytics collected via cookies when browsing our digital services.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is utilized solely to:

  • Process rider applications, verify legal driving credentials, and allocate vehicles.
  • Operate our automated battery swapping infrastructure and authenticate dock release.
  • Deliver 24/7 roadside assistance, emergency response, and preventative vehicle servicing.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, vehicle theft, or breach of safety protocols.
  • Comply with Indian regulatory, motor vehicle, and corporate laws.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption (AES-256 and TLS 1.3) across all stored and in-transit data. Access to rider records is restricted to authorized personnel. Telemetry data is retained in accordance with statutory requirements and securely purged thereafter.
