1. Agreement to Terms

By applying for, renting, or operating an EXTER electric vehicle or utilizing EXTER automated battery swapping hubs, you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms and Conditions. If you do not agree, you must not use our mobility products or network services.

2. Rider Eligibility & Operation

To operate an EXTER electric vehicle, the rider must:

  • Be at least 18 years of age and hold a valid Indian 2-wheeler driving license.
  • Always wear an approved safety helmet while operating the vehicle.
  • Never allow third parties or unverified persons to ride or operate the vehicle.
  • Operate the vehicle in compliance with all Indian Motor Vehicles regulations and speed limits.

3. Battery Swapping & Station Safety

Riders are permitted to swap batteries exclusively at authorized EXTER swapping hubs. Modifying, opening, or attempting to charge an EXTER battery pack via third-party wall sockets or unapproved chargers is strictly prohibited and constitutes an immediate termination of service and forfeiture of deposit.

4. Rider Rental Policy & Terms

For the dedicated visual breakdown and FAQs, visit our Rental Policy & Transparency Page

Security Deposit & Weekly Recharge

Security Deposit: A security deposit of ₹1,011/- is payable during rider onboarding. The final deposit amount may vary depending on the applicable offers and promotional plans.
Weekly Recharge: ₹1,869/- per week.
Service Fee: ₹369/- will be charged as a service fee from the security deposit. The stated refundable balance is ₹1,500/-, subject to the refund conditions in this policy.

Mandatory Documents

The following documents are compulsory before a vehicle is handed over:

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Two Bank Cheques

Security Deposit Refund Policy

The stated refund amount depends on the rental duration and the applicable deboarding conditions:

Rental Duration Stated Refund
Within 7 days No refund
45 days ₹1,500
More than 45 days ₹1,000

Weekly Billing Cycle

The billing cycle starts on the date the rider is onboarded. The billing start date is the same whether onboarding takes place in the morning or evening. Weekly recharge payments must be made on time to continue using the vehicle.

Vehicle Pickup & Deboarding Location

Vehicle pickup and onboarding must be completed at the designated 7th Phase JP Nagar Godown. Deboarding and vehicle return must also be completed at the same designated godown.
Hub Deboarding Policy: Riders must not leave or return the vehicle at another battery-swapping hub or an unauthorised location when deboarding. If the vehicle is left at any hub other than the designated godown, no security deposit refund will be provided, as per the stated policy.

Vehicle Inspection & Handover

Riders must take photographs and videos of the vehicle at the time of handover. The vehicle's condition should also be documented whenever it is handed over for servicing or inspection. Any existing damage, new damage or other discrepancy must be reported to the EXTER team promptly.

Roadside Assistance (RSA) & Vehicle Servicing

Roadside Assistance (RSA): Roadside Assistance will be provided within a 6 km radius of the designated godown or applicable hub.
Vehicle Servicing & Transportation: If a vehicle issue cannot be resolved through Roadside Assistance, the vehicle must be transported to the designated godown through Porter. Porter transportation charges must be paid by the rider.

Non-Payment & Vehicle Recovery

Riders must complete weekly recharge payments on time. In the event of non-payment, EXTER may initiate vehicle recovery. The security deposit may be forfeited in accordance with the applicable rental agreement and outstanding dues.

Refund & Document Return Timeline

Applicable refund amounts and submitted documents will be returned within 7 days after the deboarding date, subject to vehicle inspection, outstanding payment verification and completion of the deboarding process.
