Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Find immediate answers to common questions about rentals, battery swapping, deposits, and partnership terms.

How quickly can I get an EXTER electric scooter after applying?

Once you submit your application and KYC documents (Aadhaar & Driving License), verification is typically completed within 2 to 4 business hours. You can collect your vehicle the very same day from your chosen hub.

How does the refundable security deposit work?

The security deposit is strictly held in escrow during your rental period. Upon returning the scooter and clearing any outstanding weekly dues, your entire deposit is credited directly to your registered bank account via UPI within 48 hours.

What happens if a battery station has a technical malfunction?

All EXTER hubs are cloud-monitored 24/7. In the rare event a dock encounters an error, the automated system reroutes your unlock to an alternative live bay. You can also dial our 24/7 helpline at +91 80502 63303 for instant technician assistance.

Can business delivery fleets customize rental terms?

Yes. We offer custom commercial fleet electrification packages for e-commerce, quick-commerce, and logistics operators with dedicated telemetry portals, customized branding, priority swapping docks, and volume-tiered billing.
