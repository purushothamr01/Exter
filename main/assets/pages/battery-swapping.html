EFFORTLESS PROCESS

Swap. Ride. Repeat.

Designed for maximum convenience and rider speed. Four straightforward steps get you back on the road with 100% state-of-charge.

01
Step 01

Pull Up to Any Hub

Use the EXTER network map to navigate to your nearest 24/7 swapping station. Strategic urban placement ensures you're never more than 10 minutes away.

02
Step 02

Authenticate & Insert

Scan the QR code via your rider profile or tap your smart key. An open charging dock will automatically unlock with an illuminated green indicator.

03
Step 03

Automated Pack Ejection

The station initiates instant cloud telemetry verification, evaluates the incoming pack, and ejects a fully charged 100% capacity smart battery in seconds.

04
Step 04

Instant Resumption

Slot the fresh battery pack into your EXTER electric vehicle. Hear the safety latch click into place, hit the throttle, and continue your deliveries.

PATENTED HARDWARE & TELEMETRY

Engineered for Endurance. Protected by Intelligence.

Every EXTER energy pack is more than just cells in an aluminum enclosure — it is a connected computing device that constantly protects itself and reports its health to the cloud.

32 SENSORS · 500MS

Embedded Smart BMS

Proprietary Battery Management System monitors 32 internal parameters every 500 milliseconds — including cell voltages, current draws, impedance, and cycle count.

  • Active millivolt cell balancing
  • Automated microsecond short-circuit cutoff
  • Overcharge & deep discharge defense
PHASE-CHANGE SHIELD

Active Thermal Isolation

Phase-change thermal barriers and internal airflow channels prevent heat accumulation, even during high-ambient tropical summers and rapid-acceleration heavy deliveries.

  • Multi-point internal temperature probes
  • Flame-retardant structural compounds
  • Zero thermal runaway propagation
4G/LTE FLEET LINK

IoT Cloud Telemetry

Continuous 4G/LTE connectivity streams state-of-health (SoH) diagnostics to our central fleet command. Degraded packs are automatically quarantined before reaching riders.

  • Predictive maintenance machine learning
  • Tamper tracking and geofenced locking
  • Optimized algorithmic charging profiles
